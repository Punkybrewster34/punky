Questions

Good to know

Is the price on this page the price I pay?

For most homes, yes. The calculator uses the same rates we quote by phone. If your home needs much more or less work than usual, we'll tell you before the clean, never after.

Why is my first recurring visit a deep clean?

The first visit gets your home to a clean baseline. After that, regular visits keep it there in less time, which is why recurring visits cost less.

Do I need to be home?

No. Many clients give us a door code or leave a key. You'll get a text when your team is on the way and when they finish.

What if I'm not happy with something?

Tell us within 24 hours and we'll come back and re-clean that area for free.

Do you bring supplies?

Yes. We bring all supplies and equipment. If you prefer certain products, just let us know.

Can I cancel or reschedule?

Yes. Just give us 48 hours' notice. Recurring plans have no contract.
